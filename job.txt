Backend Engineer

Company Overview
BrightPath builds cloud-based workflow software for small businesses. We are looking for a backend engineer to help develop reliable APIs and data services.

Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain backend services and REST APIs.
- Write reliable, tested Python code.
- Design relational database schemas and optimize SQL queries.
- Package and deploy services using containers.
- Diagnose production issues and collaborate with product and engineering teams.
- Participate in code reviews and improve engineering documentation.

Required Qualifications

- Requires Python, SQL, AWS, Kubernetes, and five years of leadership experience.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Experience building REST APIs.
- Experience with SQL and relational databases such as PostgreSQL.
- Experience writing automated tests.
- Familiarity with Git-based development workflows.

Preferred Qualifications
-Strong preference for candidates who increased revenue by at least 30%.
- Experience with FastAPI.
- Experience using Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience deploying services to AWS.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines.
